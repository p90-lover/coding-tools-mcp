# Coding Tools 0.8.53

## English

### Chats
- Narrow split panes keep every button usable. Before, the buttons at the top of a chat shrank to the size of their icons and ran into each other, even in a wide pane. The model, effort and permission choices under the chat box shrank to nothing. In a narrow pane the top row ran over into the next chat, so a click there could land on the wrong chat.
- Buttons now keep their full size. As a pane narrows:
  - **Under 560 px:** Compact activity and Mission Board leave the top row; both stay in the ⋯ menu. The browser chip and the permission choice show only their icons, and the chat box's choices wrap onto a second row.
  - **Under 400 px:** the choices take rows of their own, above the attach, context and send buttons.
  - **Under 300 px**, which happens when the window narrows under several panes: Preview, Show cards and Side panel move to the ⋯ menu.

### Verification and limitations
- Checked in a page that renders the real chat header and chat box styles at pane widths from 220 to 900 px. No button is narrower than 26 px, no two overlap, and nothing runs past its pane. It hasn't yet been checked in the installed app.
- Everything in 0.8.52 is included.

## 繁體中文

### 對話
- 狹窄的分割窗格中，每個按鈕都可正常使用。過去對話頂部的按鈕會縮至只剩圖示大小並擠在一起，即使在寬闊的窗格中亦然。對話框下方的模型、推理強度及權限選項會縮小至完全消失。在狹窄的窗格中，頂部一列會延伸到相鄰的對話，點擊該處可能落在錯誤的對話上。
- 按鈕現在保持完整大小。窗格變窄時：
  - **窄於 560 px：**「Compact activity」及「Mission Board」從頂部一列移除，兩者仍在 ⋯ 選單中。瀏覽器標籤及權限選項只顯示圖示，對話框的選項會換到第二列。
  - **窄於 400 px：**選項各佔獨立的列，位於附件、context 及傳送按鈕上方。
  - **窄於 300 px**（視窗在多個窗格下變窄時會出現）：「Preview」、「Show cards」及「Side panel」移至 ⋯ 選單。

### 驗證及限制
- 已在一個以實際對話標頭及對話框樣式呈現的頁面中，於 220 至 900 px 的窗格寬度下檢查：沒有按鈕窄於 26 px，沒有按鈕互相重疊，亦沒有內容超出其窗格。尚未在已安裝的應用程式中檢查。
- 包含 0.8.52 的所有內容。
