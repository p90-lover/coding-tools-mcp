Coding Tools 0.7.0-rc.18

Updates in-app: apps on rc.17 install it by themselves once you have been away for 10 minutes and nothing is running; apps on rc.15 or rc.16 show "Update to v0.7.0-rc.18" above Settings. The portable zip updates the same way.

New:
- CPA: account usage on the CPA pages. Tokens (input, output, total), requests and an estimated cost per account and per model, over 24 hours, 7 days, 30 days or all time, refreshed every minute while open. Codex accounts show their 5-hour and weekly quota; Cline Pass keys show their state (in use, at its limit, key rejected), requests and last use. Costs are API-equivalent estimates; subscription accounts are not billed per token.
- CPA updated to CLIProxyAPI 8.0.32.
- The update bar moves while an update checks your files and downloads, instead of sitting at 0%.

Fixes:
- Chat: the model list is what CPA serves now. A model CPA dropped no longer stays in the picker as "paused by CPA for now" and then fails with "model not found".
- Chat: models from a Claude API key (and other API-key or OpenAI-compatible providers in CPA) appear in the model list.
- Chat: the model list refreshes when you open it, so a provider added in CPA shows up without a restart; up to 500 models are listed.
- Chat: plugin models (Cline Pass, CommandCode, Grok, Kimi) no longer get an effort suffix CPA can't route; Native Codex offers only the effort levels a model supports; no 400K context window is guessed for a model whose limit is unknown; a window carried over from another model is fitted to the new one.
- Chat: agents start sooner. Pre-warmed sessions no longer queue in front of your chat, the model catalog is read once per send, and CPA waits at most 30 seconds (was 5 minutes) on accounts that are cooling down.
- WebGPT: long replies (over 12 KB, about 4,000 Chinese characters) are kept instead of being discarded; a reply ChatGPT splits into parts is joined; an error no longer hides the reply; a retried card shows the earlier attempt's reply until the new one answers.
