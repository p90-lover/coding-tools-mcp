# Coding Tools v0.8.175

## English

### Antigravity models
- **Antigravity's models are offered only where Google allows them.** Google refuses requests to Antigravity that come from other agent harnesses (Claude Code, Codex, opencode, pi and the others). It answers "Resource has been exhausted (check quota)" although no quota is used up, and Google has said that using Antigravity sign-ins this way can get the accounts suspended. These models now run in the agy chat, with its own Google sign-in. Every other harness leaves them out of its model list and says why, and a saved subagent, team role or chat on one of them is refused at once with that reason instead of failing after minutes of retries.
- Direct uses that send their own short requests, like the command reviewer and model benches, keep Antigravity's models.

Includes all changes from v0.8.174.

## 繁體中文

### Antigravity 模型
- **Antigravity 的模型只在 Google 允許的地方提供。** Google 會拒絕來自其他代理工具（Claude Code、Codex、opencode、pi 等）的 Antigravity 請求，回覆「Resource has been exhausted (check quota)」，但實際上並沒有用完配額；Google 也表示以這種方式使用 Antigravity 登入資料可能導致帳戶被停權。這些模型現在在 agy 對話中執行，使用它自己的 Google 登入。其他代理工具會從模型清單中移除它們並說明原因；已儲存在這些模型上的子代理、團隊角色或對話會立即以此原因被拒絕，而不是在重試數分鐘後才失敗。
- 直接傳送自己簡短請求的用途（例如指令審查者與模型基準測試）仍可使用 Antigravity 的模型。

包含 v0.8.174 的所有變更。
