# Coding Tools 0.8.43

## English

### Smoother app: less work in the background
- A minimized window rests its chats' background checks, screen updates and Working shimmers. A minimized page still counts as visible, so they kept running: about 20% of a core in the window's page. Screens refresh as soon as the window is restored (PR #756).
- The Working shimmer steps only the shimmers on screen instead of looking up every animation on the page 30 times a second. In a long chat that lookup alone took 13% of a core (PR #756).
- A stopped chat service settles its unfinished turns by index instead of scanning every turn of every chat. After an update resumed ten chats, that scan was most of AO's CPU (PR #756).
- AO's usage tracking resolves its folders once instead of on every line a chat writes: 44% of AO's CPU on the busy hard disk (PR #756).

### Verification and limitations
- Build the Windows installer, portable ZIP and update packs from main, and confirm CI before publication.
- Not included: the draft PRs #733, #735 and #757 to #763.
- Everything in 0.8.42 is included.

## 繁體中文

### 更流暢：背景工作更少
- 視窗最小化時，對話的背景檢查、畫面更新和「處理中」閃動效果都會暫停。最小化的頁面仍被視為可見，所以它們一直在執行，佔視窗頁面約 20% 的核心。視窗還原後畫面會立即更新（PR #756）。
- 「處理中」閃動效果只推進畫面上的閃動，不再每秒 30 次查找頁面上的所有動畫。在長對話中，單是這個查找就佔 13% 的核心（PR #756）。
- 已停止的對話服務按索引處理未完成的回合，不再掃描所有對話的每個回合。更新後恢復十個對話時，這個掃描佔了 AO 大部分的 CPU（PR #756）。
- AO 的用量追蹤只解析一次資料夾，不再在對話寫入每一行時重新解析：在繁忙的硬碟上佔 AO 44% 的 CPU（PR #756）。

- 未包含：草稿 PR #733、#735 及 #757 至 #763。
