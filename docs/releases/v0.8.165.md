# Coding Tools v0.8.165

## English

### Approve for me
- **Turning off Allow quota-based reviews now stops both standalone Codex Guardian and CPA approval reviews.** Standalone reviews for another harness can use account quota even when the Codex CLI is signed into ChatGPT. Local safety checks still run; requests they can't clear wait for your approval. Turning the setting off cancels outstanding standalone Guardian work and ignores late verdicts. This does not change eligible Auto Review checks inside Codex's own ChatGPT-signed-in sessions.

### Chats
- **Old chats take far less disk space.** AO keeps one week of its raw provider event log, which only debugging uses. Tool cards older than 30 days have their long output, results and diffs trimmed to the first 4 KB, marked as trimmed; chat text and card headers stay. AO gives the freed space back to the disk in small background steps. On a 6.6 GB chat database this frees about 3 GB over a few hours and cuts growth from about 0.22 GB to about 0.07 GB a day.

Includes all changes from v0.8.164.

## 繁體中文

### 代我核准
- **關閉「允許耗用額度的審查」後，獨立 Codex Guardian 與 CPA 核准審查都會停止。** 即使 Codex CLI 已登入 ChatGPT，由其他代理送來的獨立審查仍可能耗用帳號額度。本機安全檢查仍會執行；無法在本機判斷的請求會等候你核准。關閉設定會取消尚未完成的獨立 Guardian 審查，並忽略稍後才收到的結果。這不影響使用 ChatGPT 帳號登入的 Codex 原生工作階段中符合條件的自動審查。

### 對話
- **舊對話佔用的磁碟空間大幅減少。** AO 只保留一週的原始提供者事件記錄（僅供除錯使用），將超過 30 天的工具卡片中過長的輸出、結果與差異內容裁剪為前 4 KB（並標示已裁剪；對話文字與卡片標題保持不變），並在背景以小步驟將釋出的空間歸還給磁碟。以 6.6 GB 的對話資料庫為例，約可在數小時內釋出約 3 GB，並使每日成長從約 0.22 GB 降至約 0.07 GB。

包含 v0.8.164 的所有變更。
