# Coding Tools v0.8.140

## English

### Chats: Gemini 4's full context window, and a window field that says why

- **The problem.** A Gemini 4 Carbon chat, from a Vertex AI key in CPA, was capped at a 272K context window. Its window field also turned red for "1m" and for every 7-digit size without saying why, so it looked as if sizes like "1m" or 1,000,000 weren't accepted.
- **Why.** CPA's own catalog gives every Gemini 4 model a 1,048,576-token window. Its model list, though, reports the Vertex copies (Gemini 4 Argon, Carbon and Hydrogen) at its stock 272,000, and the app treated that stock number as "unknown" only for provider-plugin models.
- **What changed.**
  - Gemini 4 Argon, Carbon, Hydrogen and Neon now get their 1,048,576-token window, so "1m" can be set. A blank chat starts at the full window.
  - A 272K that a model really has is kept: Codex's GPT models stay at 272K.
  - The custom window field accepts sizes like 100k, 1m, 1,000,000 or 250000, and its placeholder shows that. When a size can't be set it now says why, for example "Up to 272k for this model".
- **Not changed.** Gemini 4 Carbon from a Vertex key can still fail to answer: Google refuses it with "Access requires an eligible paid billing account in a supported region". That's set in the Google Cloud project, not in the app.

### Chat: the working line and the turn stats stay on one row

- In a narrow split pane, the line under a running turn ("Working for 4m · Gemini 4 Carbon — thinking · CPA refused the last 3 requests …") and the turn stats under the chat box ("6 rounds · 1014 steps | LLM … | first token … | cache … | in … out … | 4 refused …") each wrapped onto four lines.
- Both now stay on one row. Text that fits shows as before. Text that doesn't fit fades out at its end, and the whole line shows when you hover over it.
- The working line's Recover, Answer or Continue button stays in view beside the text.
- The turn stats start with CPA's refusal warning, so the cut-off end is never the warning.

## 繁體中文

### 對話：Gemini 4 的完整脈絡視窗，以及會說明原因的視窗欄位

- **問題。** 來自 CPA 中 Vertex AI 金鑰的 Gemini 4 Carbon 對話，脈絡視窗被限制在 272K。它的視窗欄位遇到「1m」和每個 7 位數大小時也會變紅，卻不說明原因，看起來就像「1m」或 1,000,000 這類大小不被接受。
- **原因。** CPA 自己的型錄為每個 Gemini 4 模型提供 1,048,576 個 token 的視窗。但它的模型清單以內建的預設值 272,000 回報 Vertex 版本（Gemini 4 Argon、Carbon 和 Hydrogen），而應用程式只對供應商外掛的模型把這個預設值視為「未知」。
- **改了什麼。**
  - Gemini 4 Argon、Carbon、Hydrogen 和 Neon 現在取得 1,048,576 個 token 的視窗，因此可以設為「1m」。空白的對話會從完整視窗開始。
  - 模型真正的 272K 會保留：Codex 的 GPT 模型維持 272K。
  - 自訂視窗欄位接受 100k、1m、1,000,000 或 250000 這類大小，提示文字也這樣顯示。無法設定的大小現在會說明原因，例如「Up to 272k for this model」。
- **沒有改變的部分。** 來自 Vertex 金鑰的 Gemini 4 Carbon 仍可能無法回應：Google 以「Access requires an eligible paid billing account in a supported region」拒絕它。這需要在 Google Cloud 專案中設定，不是在應用程式裡。

### 對話：執行中的狀態列和回合統計都維持在一行

- 在窄的分割窗格中，執行中回合下方的狀態列（「Working for 4m · Gemini 4 Carbon — thinking · CPA refused the last 3 requests …」）和對話框下方的回合統計（「6 rounds · 1014 steps | LLM … | first token … | cache … | in … out … | 4 refused …」）都會換成四行。
- 現在兩者都維持在一行。放得下的文字照常顯示；放不下的文字會在結尾淡出，滑鼠移到上面時會顯示整行內容。
- 狀態列的 Recover、Answer 或 Continue 按鈕會留在文字旁邊，不會被擠出畫面。
- 回合統計會以 CPA 的拒絕警告開頭，因此被截掉的結尾不會是警告。
