Coding Tools 0.7.0-rc.23

Updates in-app from rc.19 and later. On rc.18 or earlier, install it once by hand: Coding.Tools_0.7.0-rc.23_win_x64_portable.zip (unzip it over your portable folder, with the app closed) or Coding.Tools_0.7.0-rc.23_win_x64.exe (the installer).

Fixes:
- Chat: a model CPA has listed stays in the model list. When CPA sets it aside for a while (cooling down after upstream errors, its account disabled, CPA restarting or not reachable), it no longer shows "not available" or refuses your message: the message goes to CPA, which answers for itself. Why CPA is not listing it is in the model's tooltip. Models are remembered for 30 days after CPA last listed them, across restarts.
- Chat: the Reasoning effort menu no longer opens empty. It says why the model takes no effort setting, and offers Auto to clear an effort carried over from another model. The model keeps its context-window options.
- Chat: the effort and context-window chips keep their full text instead of shrinking to "4…" against the mic button.
- Stop always stops: the run's working agents are interrupted even when Agent Orchestrator refuses the stop. An action that fails now shows its reason instead of only "IPC_TRANSPORT_FAILED: apps.call failed".

Everything in rc.22 is included.
