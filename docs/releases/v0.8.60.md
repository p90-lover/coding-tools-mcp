# Coding Tools 0.8.60

## English

### Chats
- The goal bar keeps its layout in a narrow pane. In a chat roughly 480–560 px wide, such as a split pane or a narrowed window, the goal's text used to fit beside the buttons. "Pursuing goal" was then cut to its first letter, and the time sat on a line of its own. The state and buttons now always have the first line, and the goal and its time share the second.
- The goal's text holds one line and fades out just before its time, instead of ending in "…". Its type shrinks with the pane, from 12 px at 500 px and wider to 10 px at 375 px and narrower.
- A chat's top row (folder, name, status and buttons) fades into the chat instead of being a solid strip. The chat scrolls on under it, so a little more of it shows. Clicks in the row's empty space reach the chat, and a running plan stays pinned just below the row.

### Verification and limitations
- Measured on a test page with the app's stylesheet: at 380, 480 and 540 px the state has the first line with its buttons and the goal shares the second with its time. At 620 px all of it stays on one line, with the state shown in full. The goal is one line at every width, its text is 10.1 px at 380 px, 11.7 px at 480 px and 12 px from 540 px, and it fades out 7 px before the time. The chat's first line starts below the 48 px top row, a line scrolled under it shows through its lower half, and a running plan stops 0 px below it. Not yet checked in the installed app.
- Built and published by GitHub CI from main; no local build was made for this version.
- Everything in 0.8.59 is included.

## 繁體中文

### 對話
- 目標列在狹窄的窗格中會保持版面。在大約 480–560 px 寬的對話中（例如分割窗格或縮窄的視窗），目標文字過去會擠到按鈕旁邊，「Pursuing goal」因而只剩第一個字母，時間則獨佔一行。現在狀態和按鈕一定在第一行，目標和時間則一同在第二行。
- 目標文字保持在一行，並在時間前逐漸淡出，不再以「…」結尾。文字大小會隨窗格縮小：500 px 或以上為 12 px，375 px 或以下為 10 px。
- 對話頂部一行（資料夾、名稱、狀態及按鈕）不再是實心橫條，而是逐漸淡入對話。對話會在它下方繼續捲動，因此可以多看到一點內容。點擊這一行的空白處會落到對話上，執行中的計劃則會固定在這一行下方。

### 驗證及限制
- 在使用應用程式樣式表的測試頁面上量測：在 380、480 及 540 px 時，狀態與按鈕在第一行，目標與時間在第二行；在 620 px 時全部保持在同一行，狀態完整顯示。目標在各寬度都只有一行，文字在 380 px 時為 10.1 px、480 px 時為 11.7 px、540 px 起為 12 px，並在時間前 7 px 淡出。對話第一行從 48 px 高的頂部一行下方開始，捲到它下方的內容會透過其下半部顯示，執行中的計劃則停在它下方 0 px 處。尚未在已安裝的應用程式中檢查。
- 由 GitHub CI 從 main 建置及發佈；此版本沒有在本機建置。
- 包含 0.8.59 的所有內容。
