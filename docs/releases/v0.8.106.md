# Coding Tools 0.8.106

## English

### Error messages: top-left, past the sidebar, and gone after 5 seconds (#1150)
- **The "Something went wrong" box moves from the bottom-right to the top-left of the page,** a small gap past the sidebar and just below the title bar.
  - It follows the sidebar: past its full width when it is open, past the icon rail when it is hidden, and at the window's edge in the narrow layout. It glides along when the sidebar opens or closes.
- **It now fades out by itself after 5 seconds.** Until now it stayed up until you clicked Dismiss or did something else.
  - Pointing at it keeps it up. Moving away gives it another 5 seconds.
  - A new error replaces it and starts the 5 seconds again.

### Chats: coming back to an idle chat no longer takes 1–2 minutes (#1148)
- **A chat's agent is stopped after 30 minutes idle and brought back when you send the next message.**
  - Bringing it back used to re-import the chat's whole history one database write at a time. On a busy disk, a long chat took one to three minutes, and often ended with a new agent and a summary instead of the chat's own agent.
  - The history now goes in batches. In a timing run on the same disk this was five times faster: 3,002 events in 27 s instead of 2 min 18 s.
  - The app also starts bringing the agent back while it is still preparing the message, not after.

### Activity log: a slow agent start names its cause (#1147)
- **When an agent is slow to take a message, the app's log now says why:**
  - AO's report of who holds its database writer (`ao.writer_held`);
  - any stage of an agent's start that took 5 s or more (`ao.start_stage_slow`).
- Both stay in memory only.

### Verification and limitations
- GitHub Actions is unavailable for this repository. This release was built, tested and published locally from the merged `main`, following the release workflow's steps.
- #1150: renderer typecheck passes. The 20 desktop test files that read `App.tsx` or `styles.css` pass 465 tests; one, `localization.test.cjs`, could not load in the sparse worktree and reads only `runtime-web` files. In a browser preview of the real `App` with a fake launcher bridge, I measured:
  - the toast at 12 px past a 252 px sidebar and 12 px below the title bar;
  - 12 px past the 52 px rail with the sidebar hidden;
  - gone 5.9 s after it appeared;
  - still up after 7.5 s while hovered, and gone 6.1 s after the pointer left.
  - Not checked in the packaged window.
- #1148: resumed chats were timed on the user's machine. Small chats took 8–17 s; a 28 MB chat took 76 s to over 170 s before the fix. A new AO test imports a 304-event history in 3 transactions and fails on the old code. Workflow tests: 525/526 overall, and 502/502 in the 23 files that read the workflow source. It moves `module/agent-orchestrator` to 09d1a678d (agent-orchestrator#24), and the AO daemon is rebuilt from that pin.
- #1147: desktop-electron only (`ao-daemon-log.cjs`, `agent-orchestrator-upstream.cjs` and their tests). No AO change.
- Includes everything in 0.8.105.

## 繁體中文

### 錯誤訊息：移到左上角、側邊欄旁，5 秒後自動消失（#1150）
- **「Something went wrong」訊息框由右下角移到頁面左上角，**與側邊欄保持一小段間距，位於標題列下方。
  - 它會跟隨側邊欄：側邊欄展開時排在完整寬度之後，收起時排在圖示欄之後，窄版面時則貼近視窗邊緣。側邊欄開合時它會一同滑動。
- **現在會在 5 秒後自動淡出。** 之前它會一直顯示，直到你按下 Dismiss 或進行其他操作。
  - 滑鼠指向它時會保持顯示；移開後再顯示 5 秒。
  - 出現新的錯誤時會取代它，並重新計算 5 秒。

### 對話：回到閒置的對話不再需要等 1 至 2 分鐘（#1148）
- **對話的代理閒置 30 分鐘後會停止，傳送下一則訊息時再重新啟動。**
  - 以往重新啟動時，系統會把整段對話紀錄逐筆寫回資料庫。在忙碌的磁碟上，較長的對話要花一到三分鐘，最後還常常改由新代理接手並附上摘要，而不是原本的代理。
  - 現在對話紀錄改為分批寫入。在同一顆磁碟上的計時測試快了五倍：3,002 筆事件由 2 分 18 秒縮短為 27 秒。
  - 應用程式在準備訊息的同時就開始喚回代理，不必等準備完成。

### 活動記錄：代理啟動緩慢時會指出原因（#1147）
- **代理遲遲未接收訊息時，應用程式記錄現在會指出原因：**
  - AO 回報目前由誰占用其資料庫寫入權（`ao.writer_held`）；
  - 代理啟動時任何耗時 5 秒以上的階段（`ao.start_stage_slow`）。
- 兩者都只保留在記憶體中。
