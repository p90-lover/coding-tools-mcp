# Coding Tools 0.8.30

## English

### Improvements
- The chat freezes far less on long transcripts. Each chat pane is now its own layout root, so a change in one pane no longer re-lays out the whole window. Measured live, layout time fell from about 0.6 s to 0.02 s per second with five panes open (PR #643). The annotation layer also skips its layout reads when it has nothing to draw (PR #646).
- Opening a chat no longer makes its polls fail with "HEADLESS_CAPACITY: request admission is full". Finished turns are now read back three at a time, and a turn requested twice is read once. Before, they were all read at once and took every request slot of the local service (PR #645).
- "Retry automatically" keeps going after three quick tries. A failed step now gets up to eight tries over about half an hour, with pauses that grow from 10 seconds to 15 minutes. Before, it stopped after a minute and waited for a click that would have worked (PR #647).
- Ended turns show how long they worked and their own requests. The answering agent's work stays under "Worked" when a mission ends (PR #642, #644).
- CPA Auth Files shows Cline Pass usage windows and Codex credits (PR #642).
- ProxyBridge Nova runs the Proxy page and its routes can pick Nova's proxies. CPA routes are checked, and an agent gets 90 s to resume instead of 6 minutes (PR #641).

### Verification and limitations
- Build the Windows installer, portable ZIP and update packs from this branch and confirm CI before publication.
- Everything in 0.8.29 is included.

## 繁體中文

### 更新內容
- 長對話卡頓大幅減少：每個對話窗格現在是獨立的版面根，一個窗格有變動時不會再令整個視窗重新排版。實測開啟五個窗格時，排版時間由每秒約 0.6 秒降至 0.02 秒（PR #643）。標註層沒有內容要繪製時，也會略過版面讀取（PR #646）。
- 開啟對話時，輪詢不會再出現「HEADLESS_CAPACITY: request admission is full」錯誤：已結束的回合現在每次讀回三個，同一回合被要求兩次只會讀一次。之前所有回合會同時讀取，佔用本機服務的全部請求名額（PR #645）。
- 「自動重試」在三次快速重試後會繼續：失敗的步驟現在最多重試八次，歷時約半小時，間隔由 10 秒逐步增至 15 分鐘。之前約一分鐘後便停止，要等使用者按下重試，而按下後其實可以成功（PR #647）。
- 已結束的回合會顯示工作時間及其請求；任務結束時，負責回答的代理的工作會保留在「Worked」之下（PR #642、#644）。
- CPA 的 Auth Files 會顯示 Cline Pass 用量時段及 Codex 額度（PR #642）。
- ProxyBridge Nova 負責代理頁面，路由可選用 Nova 的代理；CPA 路由會被檢查，代理恢復的等待時間由 6 分鐘縮短至 90 秒（PR #641）。

### 驗證及限制
- Windows 安裝程式、免安裝 ZIP 和更新檔需由本分支編譯及通過 CI 才能公開。
- 包含 0.8.29 的所有內容。
