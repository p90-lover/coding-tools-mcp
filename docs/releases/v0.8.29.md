# Coding Tools 0.8.29

## English

### Improvements
- WebGPT no longer fails every turn with "listen EADDRINUSE … codex-chatgpt-web". A bridge that shuts down now closes its open broker connections, so it exits and frees the pipe for the next one. A bridge that can't claim the pipe tries again on the next turn instead of repeating the failure (PR #631).
- The chat no longer freezes on long transcripts. Thought and tool rows from finished turns stopped shimmering. Over a hundred of them were repainting every frame and keeping the window busy (PR #637).
- A running chat's spinner in the chat list is clearly visible, with a thicker ring and a darker turning arc (PR #636).
- Native chat models are kept, and chats owned by the Codex bridge recover after a restart (PR #633).
- CPA quota stays on screen, refreshing every 2 to 5 minutes, and split view keeps its panes in place when moved (PR #634).
- A restored WebGPT chat takes its thread and turn from the request header when its saved metadata is missing or partial, and metadata that conflicts with the header is refused instead of rebinding the chat to another task (PR #630).
- WebGPT keeps rich replies (tables, links, code) in the chat, and tasks delegated from the Codex app reach the model with their own instructions (PR #632).
- A runtime whose broker pipe is held by another bridge reports it on its health check and refuses WebGPT turns until the pipe is free, and stale composer drafts are no longer sent (PR #635). A broker held past its whole retry window recovers once the pipe is free (PR #638).

### Verification and limitations
- Build the Windows installer, portable ZIP and update packs from this branch and confirm CI before publication.
- Everything in 0.8.28 is included.

## 繁體中文

### 更新內容
- WebGPT 不會再因「listen EADDRINUSE … codex-chatgpt-web」而令每個回合都失敗：橋接程式關閉時會先結束仍開啟的連線，因此能正常退出並釋放管道給下一個橋接程式；未能取得管道的橋接程式會在下一回合重試，不會一直重複同一個錯誤（PR #631）。
- 長對話不再卡頓：已結束回合中的思考及工具列不再閃爍。之前有一百多列在每一格畫面都重新繪製，令視窗一直忙碌（PR #637）。
- 對話清單中運行中對話的旋轉圖示更清楚：圓環更粗，轉動的弧線顏色更深（PR #636）。
- 原生對話的模型會被保留，由 Codex 橋接管理的對話在重新啟動後可以恢復（PR #633）。
- CPA 額度會保持顯示，每 2 至 5 分鐘更新一次；分割檢視移動時，各窗格會維持在原位（PR #634）。
- 恢復的 WebGPT 對話在已儲存資料缺少或不完整時，會從請求標頭取得對話及回合資料；與標頭衝突的資料會被拒絕，不會把對話綁定到另一個任務（PR #630）。
- WebGPT 的豐富回覆（表格、連結、程式碼）會完整保留在對話中，由 Codex 應用程式委派的任務會連同其指示傳給模型（PR #632）。
- 當代理管道被另一個橋接程式佔用時，執行環境會在健康檢查中顯示，並在管道釋放前拒絕 WebGPT 回合；過時的輸入框草稿不會再被送出（PR #635）。即使管道被佔用超過整個重試時間，釋放後代理仍可恢復（PR #638）。
