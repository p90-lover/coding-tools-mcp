# Coding Tools v0.8.139

## English

### Less lag while the pet naps

- **The problem.** With the app window open, its page kept a core busy even when nothing was happening, and the computer felt slow. The window drew about 93 frames a second, each repainting a 63,000-element page, and sent the graphics process about 1.3 GB a minute.
- **Why.** The pet sleeping in its cottage shows three rising "z" marks. They looped at the screen's full refresh rate, so the whole page redrew at that rate. The app already slows its other looping marks (the working shimmers, pulsing dots and spinners) to 10–30 steps a second; the pet's z's and its chimney smoke were left out.
- **What changed.** The pet's z's and smoke now step with the other marks. They look the same, and the window no longer redraws at full speed while the pet naps.

### Verification

- Measured on the running 0.8.138 app, pausing only the pet's z's for 30 seconds: the page's traffic to the graphics process went from 1,345 to 438 MB a minute, the page from 95% to 80% of a core, and the graphics process from 46% to 30%.
- Giving the z's their own GPU layer instead changed nothing (1,224 MB a minute), so the cost was the frame rate they forced, not their drawing.
- New test: the napping pet's z's and smoke are paused and stepped on the shared clock. It fails on the old code. All 11 ticker tests pass.
- Everything in 0.8.138 is included.

## 繁體中文

### 寵物小睡時不再拖慢電腦

- **問題。** 開著應用程式視窗時，即使沒有任何動作，頁面仍持續佔用一個 CPU 核心，電腦感覺變慢。視窗每秒繪製約 93 個畫面，每次都重繪一個有 63,000 個元素的頁面，並每分鐘傳送約 1.3 GB 給圖形處理程序。
- **原因。** 寵物在小屋裡睡覺時會顯示三個往上飄的「z」。它們以螢幕的完整更新率循環，因此整個頁面也以這個速度重繪。應用程式原本就會把其他循環標記（工作中的閃光、跳動的圓點與轉圈）放慢到每秒 10–30 步，但寵物的 z 和煙囪的煙被漏掉了。
- **變更。** 寵物的 z 和煙現在與其他標記一起逐步更新。外觀不變，寵物小睡時視窗也不再全速重繪。

### 驗證

- 在執行中的 0.8.138 上量測，只暫停寵物的 z 30 秒：頁面傳給圖形處理程序的資料由每分鐘 1,345 MB 降到 438 MB，頁面由 95% 降到 80% 個核心，圖形處理程序由 46% 降到 30%。
- 改為讓 z 擁有自己的 GPU 圖層則沒有任何改善（每分鐘 1,224 MB），所以成本來自它們強制的畫面更新率，而不是繪製本身。
- 新測試：寵物小睡時的 z 和煙會被暫停，並依共用時鐘逐步更新。此測試在舊程式碼上失敗。所有 11 項閃光計時器測試皆通過。
- 包含 0.8.138 的所有內容。
